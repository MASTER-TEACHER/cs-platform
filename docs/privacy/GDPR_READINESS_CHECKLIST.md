# CS Master UK GDPR / Privacy Readiness Checklist

## Implemented in this batch

- Expanded public privacy information.
- Dedicated security information page.
- Authenticated data-rights request workflow.
- Platform-admin privacy request queue.
- Direct client Firestore access to privacy/admin audit collections denied.
- Safer authentication/admin error handling so unexpected server errors are not returned verbatim.
- Strengthened defensive HTTP headers.
- DPIA working record.
- Personal-data map.
- Retention schedule framework.
- Security/personal-data incident response plan.
- Draft school data-processing schedule.
- Operational provider/subprocessor register.
- Targeted release-test checklist.

## Must be completed operationally before describing CS Master as “GDPR compliant”

- Identify the legal/operator entity and publish its required identity/contact details.
- Confirm whether an ICO data-protection fee/registration obligation applies.
- Approve documented lawful bases by processing purpose.
- Finalise controller/processor allocation with school legal terms.
- Obtain/retain current DPAs and transfer terms for every relevant provider.
- Confirm hosting/database/service regions actually configured in production.
- Adopt concrete retention periods and implement deletion/offboarding procedures.
- Define who owns privacy requests and response deadlines.
- Define children/young-person transparency and age-assurance approach appropriate to the service.
- Verify AI-provider data-use/retention settings under the actual account contract.
- Review whether any automated decision-making/profiling engages additional legal requirements.
- Carry out school/DPO and appropriate learner consultation for the DPIA where proportionate.
- Have final Privacy Notice, DPA and school contract legally reviewed.
- Keep records of processing activities appropriate to the organisation.
- Run a security incident tabletop exercise and key-rotation test.
