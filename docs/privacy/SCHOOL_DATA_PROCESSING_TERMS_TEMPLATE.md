# CS Master School Data Processing Terms — Draft Schedule

> Draft for legal review. This is not a signed Data Processing Agreement.

## 1. Parties and roles

The final agreement must state, for each processing purpose, whether the school is controller and CS Master processor, whether CS Master is an independent controller for separate platform purposes, or whether another allocation applies. Contract labels must match actual processing.

## 2. Subject matter and duration

Provision of CS Master learning, assessment, class, analytics, support and school-management functionality for the subscription/trial period plus the agreed secure offboarding/deletion period.

## 3. Nature and purpose

Hosting and processing account, class, learning, assessment and administration information to deliver the functions enabled by the school.

## 4. Data subjects

Learners, teachers, school administrators and relevant support contacts.

## 5. Categories of personal data

Account identifiers/contact data, school/class membership, curriculum/progress, assignments, assessment/programming responses, marks/feedback, interventions/analytics, integrity events, and support records as applicable.

## 6. Processor commitments where CS Master acts as processor

Include contractual terms covering:
- processing only on documented instructions;
- confidentiality;
- appropriate technical/organisational measures;
- subprocessor controls;
- assistance with data-subject requests;
- breach/security assistance and notification;
- DPIA/regulatory assistance where required;
- return/deletion at end of service;
- information/audit rights as required by law;
- international transfer safeguards.

## 7. Security schedule

Reference authenticated access, role/tenant controls, server-side privileged operations, encryption in transit, secret management, backup/provider controls, incident response and security testing.

## 8. Subprocessors

Attach the current validated subprocessor register and define advance-change notification/objection procedure.

## 9. School responsibilities

The school remains responsible for lawful instructions, account administration, appropriate learner/staff access, its own notices, handling controller decisions, and timely offboarding instructions where applicable.

## 10. AI and monitored assessment

Document which AI features are enabled, what context may be transmitted, human-review expectations and the position on use of learner information for provider/model training. Disclose monitored Exam Mode integrity events and ensure school policies support proportionate use.

## 11. End of contract

Define export window, deletion instruction, account access cessation, backup expiry and any records retained under an independent legal obligation.
